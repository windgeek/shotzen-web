ShotZen

Privacy Policy · Effective 7 August 2026

ShotZen collects no data. Not a reduced amount, not an anonymised amount — none. A tool that watches your screen has no business doing anything else.

What is collected

Nothing. ShotZen has no account and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no identifiers of any kind. It does not read your contacts, your location, or any other personal data.

Why it cannot send anything

This is not only a promise. ShotZen runs in Apple's App Sandbox and ships without the network entitlement, so macOS itself refuses to open an outbound connection on its behalf. There is no server to receive your data because the app cannot reach one.

Screen Recording permission

macOS requires this permission of every screenshot tool; without it the system returns a blank image. ShotZen reads the screen only in response to a capture you start yourself — a shortcut, a menu bar command, or the delayed capture you asked for. It does not observe or record in the background.

The pixels of a capture live in memory, go to your clipboard, and — if you turned that on — to the folder you chose. Nowhere else.

Files on your Mac

Because ShotZen is sandboxed, it can write only to the folder you picked yourself. That choice is remembered as a macOS security-scoped bookmark so you are not asked again; it grants access to that folder and nothing else.

Your settings

Shortcuts, save location, language, and the rest are stored in macOS's own preferences on your Mac. They are never transmitted, and no one but you can read them.

Third parties

ShotZen embeds no third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics providers. Nothing is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because nothing is gathered in the first place.

Children

No data is collected from anyone, at any age. There is nothing for a child to disclose.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that affected what the app collects would also mean a new version of the app on the App Store.

macOS verlangt sie von jedem Bildschirmfoto-Programm; ohne sie liefert das System ein leeres Bild. Die Bildpunkte einer Aufnahme liegen im Arbeitsspeicher, gehen in Ihre Zwischenablage und — falls Sie das eingeschaltet haben — in den von Ihnen gewählten Ordner. Sonst nirgendwohin.
